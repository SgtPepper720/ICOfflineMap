IC Offline Map

Offline-friendly mapping for inclusive churches.

This web app provides an offline-friendly map of Inclusive Church locations in the UK, where the full map is downloaded to your device (around 100MB).

Church information is based on the Inclusive Church database, which is undergoing updates and cleanup at this time.

To install this to your phone, go to your browser menu and select "Add to Home Screen" (or similar). You can then use the app offline, and refresh the data when you have an internet connection.

Install this map as an app

Use the steps below to put the offline map on your device and reopen it quickly.

📶 Offline ready 🏠 Home screen 🪟 Taskbar
Safari on iPhone or iPad ▾
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Give it a name and tap Add.
Pin it: the app icon appears on your Home Screen. Press and hold it to move it to another page or keep it in your Dock for even faster access.
Chrome on Android ▾
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button and choose Install app or Add to Home screen if that option is shown.
  3. Confirm the prompt to place the app icon on your device.
Pin it: once installed, the icon stays on your Home Screen or in your app drawer. Long-press it and drag it to your preferred position.
Microsoft Edge on Windows ▾
  1. Open this page in Edge.
  2. Click the three-dot menu and go to Apps > Install this site as an app.
  3. Confirm the install and allow it to open as a standalone app.
Pin it: the app can be pinned to your taskbar or Start menu from the installed app icon, which makes it easier to open again later.
